# Parts Arrive at the Receiving Dock

Part of [[QE Receiving Workflow]] · start · [[W02 - Paperwork Gathered and Filed to the PO Folder|W02 →]]

**Who:** Operations  
**Where:** Receiving Dock (SOP 7.4.3 §4.2)

## Physical work
- Carrier delivers the pallet or boxes; they sit on the dock.
- The vendor's packing slip (and sometimes certs) ships with the goods.

## Records
- Vendor packing slip

## Automation embedded in this step
- None. This step is entirely physical or manual.

## Watch-outs
- SOP 7.4.3 Table 1, step 1: goods are to be handled within **16 working hours** of being received. A Friday delivery that waits for Monday should already be received into Quarantine (W03) inside that window.
